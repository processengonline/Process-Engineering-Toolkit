Control Valve / RO Noise Screening

Order-of-magnitude aerodynamic (gas/vapor) and hydrodynamic (liquid/cavitation) noise screening for control valve trim and restriction-orifice plates — exact choked/subcritical Mach numbers, downstream pipe Mach, mechanical stream power, and the cavitation index σ, banded against well-known industry thresholds to flag when low-noise/anti-cavitation trim or a certified vendor calculation is needed.

Screening tool only — not a certified IEC 60534-8-3 / 8-4 noise calculation. IEC 60534-8-3 (aerodynamic) and IEC 60534-8-4 (hydrodynamic/cavitation) noise prediction use dozens of empirical, valve-style- and trim-geometry-specific coefficients (Fd, FL, xFz, valve-style correction factors, multi-hole/multi-stage trim curves) that cannot be reproduced reliably from first principles or from memory. This tool instead computes the physically unambiguous quantities exactly — choked/subcritical throat Mach number, downstream pipe Mach number, mechanical stream power, cavitation index σ — and bands them against genuine, widely-used industry rule-of-thumb thresholds (pressure ratio >4:1, pipe Mach >0.5, σ < 1.5, etc.). The dB sound-level figure on the gas/vapor tab is an explicit order-of-magnitude estimate carrying a ±15 dB uncertainty band — illustrative for ranking severity, not a design number. Any trim flagged MODERATE or HIGH here still requires the valve/RO vendor's certified IEC 60534-8-3 / 8-4 calculation, run with their trim's actual Fd/FL/xFz and acoustic test data, before final trim selection.

Liquid — cavitation index σ (hydrodynamic noise screening): P1abs = P1barg + 1.01325; P2abs = P1abs − ΔP; σ = (P1abs − Pv) / (P1abs − P2abs). Restriction velocity v = Q/(3600·A) shown for context only, A entered as effective flow area (mm²). Flashing is flagged directly whenever P2abs ≤ Pv (guaranteed vapor formation at/after the restriction) regardless of σ.
Liquid risk bands (general industry screening guideline, not a certified limit): σ > 2 — LOW risk, comfortable incipient-cavitation margin. 1.5 – 2 — MODERATE, monitor and confirm against valve-specific FL/xFz data. σ < 1.5 — HIGH, likely cavitating/flashing service — anti-cavitation trim or multi-stage pressure letdown indicated. These thresholds are a general screening rule; actual incipient/damage cavitation limits are trim-geometry-specific and require vendor Kc/xFz data for final trim selection.
Gas/vapor — choked vs. subcritical: Pratio = P2abs/P1abs; critical ratio Pc = (2/(k+1))^(k/(k−1)). Choked when Pratio ≤ Pc: the jet is sonic at the vena contracta by definition (Mach = 1 exactly) — throat temperature T* = T1·2/(k+1), sonic velocity c* = √(k·Z·Rspec·T*). Subcritical (Pratio > Pc): isentropic throat temperature Tthroat = T1·Pratio^((k−1)/k), throat density ρthroat = P2abs/(Z·Rspec·Tthroat), velocity from continuity v = ṁ/(ρthroat·A), Mach Mj = v/√(k·Z·Rspec·Tthroat) — verified numerically to approach 1.000 as Pratio→Pc and to reduce exactly to the choked sonic-velocity result at the critical ratio.
Downstream pipe Mach: ρ2 = P2abs/(Z2·Rspec·T1) — evaluated at T1 (little temperature recovery assumed downstream, a reasonable screening simplification, not a full energy balance). vpipe = ṁ/(ρ2·Apipe); Machpipe = vpipe/√(k·Z2·Rspec·T1). Bands: <0.3 OK · 0.3–0.5 CAUTION (velocity-related noise/vibration/erosion risk, consider a larger downstream line) · >0.5 WARN (high risk, redesign downstream piping).
Total pressure ratio risk band (P1/P2): <2 — LOW, single-stage trim typically adequate. 2–4 — MODERATE, single-stage trim typically adequate, confirm with vendor. >4 — HIGH, multi-stage/low-noise trim strongly indicated. This is the same >4:1 physical driver as the multi-stage advisory in the companion Restriction Orifice calculator (there flagged as P2/P1 < 0.25) — both point at the same choked, high-pressure-drop-ratio regime that dominates aerodynamic noise generation.
Mechanical stream power (exact, low-risk quantity): W = ṁ(kg/s)·ΔP(Pa)/ρavg, ρavg = ½(ρ1+ρ2), ρ1 = P1abs/(Z1·Rspec·T1). This is the rate of mechanical energy dissipated across the restriction — an unambiguous physical quantity, not itself a noise level.
Order-of-magnitude internal sound power (illustrative, NOT the IEC 60534-8-3 regime formula): Lwi ≈ 10·log₁₀(η·W / 10⁻¹²) dB, with acoustic efficiency η taken as a round order-of-magnitude value scaling with the same pressure-ratio band above: η ≈ 10⁻⁷ (P1/P2 < 2, low), 10⁻⁵ (2–4, moderate), 10⁻⁴ (>4, high/choked-dominated). These η values are illustrative round numbers for ranking severity before a certified calculation — not the trim-style-specific efficiency terms of IEC 60534-8-3.
External SPL estimate (±15 dB): SPLest ≈ Lwi − TL, TL = user-entered assumed pipe transmission loss (default 20 dB). Displayed as the range [SPLest − 15, SPLest + 15] dB, never as a single number — this conflates sound power and sound pressure level as a deliberate simplification for order-of-magnitude ranking only. Final noise-critical trim selection requires the vendor's certified IEC 60534-8-3/8-4 calculation with their trim's actual Fd and acoustic test data.
Z-factor: reuses the Dranchuk–Abou-Kassem (1975) correlation and Standing pseudo-critical-from-gas-gravity method [Tpc(°R) = 168+325·SG−12.5·SG², Ppc(psia) = 677+15·SG−37.5·SG², SG = MW/28.9647] from the companion Restriction Orifice / Compressor Settle-Out calculators — ideal (Z=1), gas-gravity estimate, or direct Pc/Tc entry.
Rspec = Runiversal/MW, Runiversal = 8314.462618 J/(kmol·K).

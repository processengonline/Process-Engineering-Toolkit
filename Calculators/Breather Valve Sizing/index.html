Inputs — Incompressible Orifice

Q — total liquid flow through the orifice.
ρ — e.g. water ≈ 1000 kg/m³.
ΔP — upstream minus downstream pressure.
Typical range 0.6 – 0.8 for a sharp-edged orifice.

Inputs — Choked Ideal-Gas Orifice

ṁ — required mass flow through the valve.
P₀ — upstream stagnation pressure.
T₀ — upstream absolute temperature.
Default 0.02897 kg/mol = air.
Default 1.4 for air / diatomic gas.
Typical range 0.7 – 0.95 for sharp-edged orifices.
Valid only when downstream/upstream pressure ratio is at or below the critical (choked) ratio.

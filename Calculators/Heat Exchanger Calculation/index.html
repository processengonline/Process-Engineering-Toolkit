Heat Exchanger Duty & LMTD Sizing — Screening Tool

Shell & Tube / Air Cooler Duty & LMTD Sizing Calculator

First-pass thermal sizing: duty from terminal temperatures, LMTD with the Bowman (1940) 1-shell/2-tube-pass F-factor correction for shell-and-tube, and process-side/air-side duty, cross-flow LMTD, face area and fan power for air-cooled exchangers. Order-of-magnitude required-area estimates only.

Rough first-pass sizing — not a rating program. This tool computes duty from the flows/temperatures you enter, LMTD (with the classical Bowman TEMA-E F-factor correction where selected), and required heat-transfer area from a user-supplied overall coefficient U. It does not perform tube-side/shell-side or air-side finned-tube film-coefficient correlations, fouling-factor buildup, pressure-drop-driven geometry selection, tube layout/baffle design, vibration checks, or multi-shell/multi-bundle configuration logic. The U values suggested here are order-of-magnitude guidance only — they vary enormously with real fluid properties, velocities, and fouling. For an actual EPC deliverable, run a certified rating program (HTRI, HTFS/Aspen EDR, or vendor air-cooler software) with real fluid property data and confirm mechanical design against TEMA / API 661.

Duty (both sides): Q = ṁ·Cp·ΔT for the appropriate side; shell & tube shows Q_hot and Q_cold independently and flags a "duty mismatch" badge when they differ by more than ~2% (a real consistency check on the entered flows/temperatures/Cp) — the sizing calc uses their average.
LMTD: counter-current pairs ΔT1 = T1−t2 (hot in vs cold out), ΔT2 = T2−t1 (hot out vs cold in); co-current pairs ΔT1 = T1−t1, ΔT2 = T2−t2. LMTD = (ΔT1−ΔT2)/ln(ΔT1/ΔT2), with LMTD = ΔT1 when ΔT1 ≈ ΔT2 (removable singularity). Both ΔT1 and ΔT2 must be > 0 — a non-positive value means the chosen arrangement can't physically achieve those terminal temperatures (a crossing), and the tool shows an explicit error rather than a bogus LMTD.
Bowman (1940) F-factor for 1 shell pass / 2 (or even multiples of 2) tube passes, TEMA-E: R = (T1−T2)/(t2−t1), P = (t2−t1)/(T1−t1). General case (R≠1): F = [√(R²+1)/(R−1)]·ln[(1−P)/(1−PR)] / ln[(2/P−1−R+√(R²+1))/(2/P−1−R−√(R²+1))]. Special case R=1 (removable singularity): F = [√2·P/(1−P)] / ln[(2/P−2+√2)/(2/P−2−√2)] (the R→1 limit of the general formula). F is always ≤ 1.0 physically — a computed F that exceeds 1.0 or is undefined (extreme/infeasible P,R) is flagged with a warning badge rather than used to produce a silently wrong area. For other shell/tube pass counts, enter F directly from the applicable TEMA/Bowman chart.
Required area (shell & tube): A = Q / (U·LMTD·F), with U a required user input (order-of-magnitude guidance only — see hint).
Air cooler duty & air flow: Q = ṁ·Cp·(T1−T2) on the process side; air outlet = ambient + ΔT_air; ṁ_air = Q / (Cp_air·ΔT_air) with Cp_air ≈ 1006 J/(kg·K) fixed.
Air cooler LMTD: computed with the counter-current formula (process hot in/out vs air in/out) as a first-pass approximation for the actual cross-flow bundle, then corrected with a user-entered cross-flow factor F_cf (typ. 0.85–0.95, confirm against API 661/vendor software).
Air density (ideal gas): ρ_air = P_atm·MW_air / (R·T), MW_air = 28.97 kg/kmol, R = 8314.462618 J/(kmol·K), T = design ambient in K — computed from the entered ambient temperature and site pressure rather than assumed fixed.
Face area: = (ṁ_air/ρ_air) / face velocity. Required (finned) area: = Q / (U·LMTD·F_cf), U referenced to bare tube OD (typ. 25–40 W/m²K, highly fin-geometry dependent).
Fan power: = (ṁ_air/ρ_air)·ΔP_air / η_fan (volumetric air flow × pressure rise ÷ combined fan/motor efficiency), reported in kW.

Control Valve Sizing — ISA-75.01.01 / IEC 60534-2-1 & -2-3

Control Valve Cv/Kv Sizing Calculator

First-pass required Kv/Cv for a control valve trim, from the IEC 60534 dimensionless sizing framework — liquid choked-flow (cavitation) screening via FL and FF, and gas/vapor choked-flow screening via xT and the expansion factor Y. Reports Kv and the equivalent Cv side by side.

Constants derived, not looked up. The IEC 60534 "N" unit-conversion constants (N1, N6, N9, …) are numerous and easy to mis-transcribe from memory. This tool avoids that risk entirely: it works from Kv's fundamental definition — the flow of water (ρ=1000 kg/m³) in m³/h through the valve at 1 bar ΔP — and derives every working equation from that definition plus dimensional consistency (SG = ρ/1000 throughout; the compressible-flow form is the same relationship extended via mass conservation, with the "1000" arising purely from the SG definition, not a separate memorized constant). See the footer for the full derivation. This is a first-pass sizing screen, not a certified selection: confirm the selected trim's actual, vendor-published FL/xT/Fp/Cv characteristic (and, for the final iteration, a piping-geometry-corrected Fp if the valve size differs from the line size) before issuing a valve datasheet.

READY
Kv definition (the basis for everything below): Kv is the flow of water (ρ=1000 kg/m³) in m³/h through the valve at 1 bar ΔP. For any liquid this gives Kv = Q(m³/h)·√(SG/ΔP(bar)), SG = ρfluid/1000. No separate "N" table constant is used — the equation follows directly from the definition.
Liquid choked-flow (cavitation) check, IEC 60534-2-1: FF = 0.96 − 0.28·√(Pv/Pc) (FF→0.96 as Pv→0, matching the well-known reference value for water near room temperature); choked ΔP = FL²·(P1 − FF·Pv); effective ΔP = min(actual ΔP, choked ΔP). If actual ΔP exceeds the choked value, flow is cavitating/choked at the vena contracta — Kv will not increase further with additional ΔP, and the capped value is used.
Liquid Kv, final: Kvrequired = Q·√(SG/ΔPeffective) / Fp. Cv = 1.156·Kv (standard US/metric flow-coefficient conversion).
Gas/vapor dimensionless framework, IEC 60534-2-3: x = ΔP/P1 (absolute); xT = terminal (choked) pressure-drop ratio, valve-style dependent; xused = min(x, xT) — flow chokes once x reaches xT, exactly analogous to the liquid FL choked-ΔP cap above; expansion factor Y = 1 − xused/(3·xT), so Ymin = 1−1/3 = 0.667 at full choke.
Gas Kv, derived (not a memorized N9 constant): in the low-x (near-incompressible) limit a gas behaves like a light liquid, so by the same Kv definition, the actual volumetric flow at inlet conditions is Q1(m³/h) = Kv·Y·√(1000·xused·P1(bar)/ρ1(kg/m³)) — substituting the liquid form Q=Kv·√(ΔP/SG), SG=ρ/1000 → Q=Kv·√(1000·ΔP/ρ), with ΔP=xused·P1 and the compressible correction Y inserted; the "1000" comes purely from the SG=ρ/1000 definition. Mass flow w(kg/h) = Q1·ρ1 = Kv·Y·√(1000·xused·P1·ρ1), so Kvrequired = w / (Fp·Y·√(1000·xused·P1·ρ1)). Fp applied the same way as the liquid tab. Cv = 1.156·Kv.
Self-consistency check (verified numerically, not just asserted): at xused=1×10⁻⁸ (Y→1 to ~9 significant figures), the gas formula above and the plain liquid formula Kv=Q·√(SG/ΔP) — with Q=w/ρ1, SG=ρ1/1000, ΔP=xused·P1 — agree to a relative difference of ~4.6×10⁻⁹, confirming the gas derivation reduces exactly to the liquid form in the incompressible limit.
Inlet gas density ρ1: ρ1 = P1abs·10⁵·MW / (Z1·R·T1), R = 8314.462618 J/(kmol·K), T1 in K, MW in g/mol (numerically = kg/kmol).
Z-factor: reuses the Dranchuk–Abou-Kassem (1975) correlation and Standing pseudo-critical-from-gas-gravity method [Tpc(°R) = 168+325·SG−12.5·SG², Ppc(psia) = 677+15·SG−37.5·SG², SG = MW/28.9647] from the companion Restriction Orifice / Compressor Settle-Out calculators — ideal (Z1=1), gas-gravity estimate, or direct Pc/Tc entry, evaluated at upstream P1/T1.
Scope note: this sizes required Kv/Cv only, for a first-pass trim selection. It does not select an actual valve/trim size from a manufacturer catalog, does not compute Fp from an iterative reducer/expander beta-ratio solve, does not evaluate noise (IEC 60534-8-3/8-4) or actuator sizing, and k (Cp/Cv) is shown for reference only — it is not required by the xT-based Kv relationship used here. Confirm the selected trim's certified Cv/xT/FL/Fp from the vendor's published capacity data before finalizing.
